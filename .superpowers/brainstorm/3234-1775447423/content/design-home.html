Approach C — Elevated polish on top of Figma

🌍 Spline Earth
Live 3D interactive Spline scene replaces static illustration. Subtle mouse-parallax: earth drifts 6px on cursor move. Same scene on both pages.
✦ Custom SVG Logo
Your SVG icon inlined in JSX (not a Figma URL). Saved to public/starlink-icon.svg. Used next to STARLINK wordmark on Home; top-right on Get Starlink.
🪟 Glass Card Shimmer
On hover, the glass card gets a soft green glow: box-shadow: 0 8px 40px rgba(111,175,143,0.25). Transition 300ms ease.
→ Nav Micro-interactions
Nav links slide right 4px + reveal arrow on hover. Underline draws from left. Staggered entrance: 0.12s / 0.24s / 0.36s delay.
📱 Responsive
≤768px: Spline moves behind content at 50% opacity, glass card becomes full-width, text scales via clamp(). Touch-friendly tap targets.
⚡ Spline Strategy
Use @splinetool/react-spline/next (SSR-safe). Wrapped in Suspense with gradient fallback. "use client" on Spline wrapper component.
